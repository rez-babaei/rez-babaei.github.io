Reza Babaei — Energy Engineer & Research Scientist, Toronto

P.Eng., CEM, CMVP, LEED GA · Energy Engineer at BGIS on the TELUS telecommunications portfolio · Research Scientist, University of Windsor

Reza Babaei is a licensed Professional Engineer (P.Eng.) based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. He works as an Energy Engineer at BGIS, supporting the national real-estate portfolio of TELUS, one of Canada's largest telecommunications companies, and is a Research Scientist with the Turbulence & Energy Laboratory at the University of Windsor.

Energy engineering in telecommunications

At BGIS, Reza Babaei delivers energy and decarbonization work across TELUS telecommunications facilities, including data centres, telecom central offices and commercial buildings. His work includes:

  • ASHRAE Level II energy audits identifying conservation measures with internal rates of return above 20% and simple paybacks under five years
  • Cooling optimization for data centres and telecom central offices: CRAC/CRAH staging, supply air temperatures, airflow management, free cooling and containment, assessed against ASHRAE thermal guidelines
  • HVAC retrofit and electrification pathways modelled in EnergyPlus using local emissions factors
  • Building automation system (BAS) commissioning and optimization so savings persist after retrofits

Decarbonization and verified savings

Before BGIS, Reza spent four years at Enerlife Consulting in Toronto as an Energy Analyst and then Energy & Decarbonization Engineer. He led deep retrofits across 15+ commercial and healthcare facilities, cutting site energy use intensity by more than 20% and carbon emissions by roughly 35% per site, and verified over 400,000 kWh of electricity and 100,000 m³ of natural gas in annual savings using IPMVP measurement and verification.

Research scientist

As a research scientist at the University of Windsor, working with Dr. David S.-K. Ting and Dr. Rupp Carriveau, Reza Babaei has published 22 peer-reviewed journal articles and 3 book chapters on green hydrogen, hybrid renewable microgrids, solar tracking, battery storage and maritime decarbonization, published in journals including Energy, International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, Fuel and Energy Reports.
